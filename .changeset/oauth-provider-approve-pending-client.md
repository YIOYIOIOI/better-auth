Applications with open dynamic client registration or CIMD discovery can now hold self-registered clients until a person approves them. Define `approvePendingClient`, and a client registered without a session or initial access token, or discovered through CIMD, is stored as pending: it cannot obtain tokens until a user accepts consent and your callback names its owner (`clientReference`). The callback can also grant `client_credentials` access, limited to scopes the user accepted, and open registration may then request that grant. A pending client must register the `authorization_code` grant. Registration responses and the public client endpoints include `pending: true`, so your consent page can tell the user. Without the option, registration and consent are unchanged, except that a CIMD metadata refresh no longer undoes a scope ceiling or disable set while the refresh was running. The option adds an optional `pending` column to `oauthClient`, so run your migration.
